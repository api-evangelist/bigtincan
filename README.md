Bigtincan is an AI-powered sales enablement platform that helps customer-facing teams deliver personalized, measurable buying experiences, combining sales content management, training and coaching (sales readiness), buyer engagement analytics, digital sales rooms and CRM content sync. Its Hub Public API (pubapi.bigtincan.com) exposes REST operations for stories, channels, tabs, files, forms, groups, users, search and events. Bigtincan's own site states that Bigtincan and Showpad are now together building a combined revenue effectiveness solution, and its trust center is branded Showpad + Bigtincan.

**Areas:** Sales Enablement · Content Management · Training · Coaching · Buyer Engagement · Analytics · CRM Integration · Digital Sales Rooms

## How this profile is made
